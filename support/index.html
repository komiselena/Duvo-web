Linking with your partner

Profile → Invite partner gives you a code that lives for one hour. Your partner enters it in the same place on their phone. Once linked, journal entries, daily answers, planned dates and your connection score appear on both phones.

Linking is optional. Every part of Duvo works on one phone — Sparks games are played by passing it back and forth.

Managing a subscription

Duvo Plus is billed by Apple, not by us. Open the Settings app, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, and you can change or cancel Duvo Plus there. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

Already subscribed on another device? Open the Duvo paywall and tap Restore purchases.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your data

Profile → Delete Account removes your account and everything stored for it, including what was shared with your partner. Deleting the app from your phone removes the local copy. Both are permanent and neither can be undone.

Notifications stopped arriving

Duvo schedules reminders on the device itself. If they stop, check Settings → Duvo → Notifications, and in Duvo check Profile → Daily reflection time. Reinstalling the app clears all scheduled reminders.

Something is wrong

Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead, along with your iPhone model and iOS version. Screenshots help a lot.
