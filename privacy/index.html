Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 September 2026

Duvo is a private journal for two people. This page says exactly what it keeps, where it goes, and what never leaves your phone.

The short version

Duvo is a private journal for two people. What you write stays between you, your partner, and the systems needed to move it between your two phones. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used to advertise to you.

Your account

Duvo signs you in anonymously. You are identified by a random account identifier issued by Firebase Authentication — no email address, phone number or password is required or collected.

What you give us

  • Your name and your partner's name, and the date your relationship started, from onboarding.
  • Your answers to the setup questions: love languages, what an ideal date looks like, what you want to focus on.
  • Journal entries: titles, notes, dates, places, tags and photographs.
  • Answers to the daily question.
  • Dates you plan, and answers given while playing Sparks.

Where it is kept

Everything is written first to a database on your own device. If you have linked with a partner, the shared parts — journal entries, daily answers, planned dates and your connection score — are copied to Google Firebase Firestore under a document that only the two of you can read. Security rules enforce this on the server; nobody else can reach it, including with the app's own configuration.

Duvo does not use iCloud or CloudKit.

Photographs

Full-resolution photographs never leave your device. When a memory is shared with your partner, Duvo makes a small preview — about 600 pixels on its longest side, up to three per memory — and sends only that. Your originals stay in Duvo's local database on your phone.

Your calendar

With your permission, Duvo writes planned dates into your device's own calendar. It does not read your existing events and does not upload anything from your calendar.

Notifications

Reminders, milestones and daily nudges are scheduled locally on your device. Duvo has no push server; their contents never leave your phone.

AI date ideas

When you ask Duvo to generate date ideas, the preferences you entered during setup are sent to OpenAI to produce suggestions. Your names, your journal entries, your photographs and your daily answers are never sent. OpenAI processes the request under its own terms and does not receive anything identifying you.

Analytics and crashes

Duvo uses Firebase Analytics to see which features get used, and Firebase Crashlytics to learn about crashes. These carry no names, no journal or answer text, no memory titles and no pairing codes — only which screen was opened and what kind of action was taken.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. Apphud helps Duvo know whether your subscription is active, including on a second device. Duvo never sees your payment details.

Deleting everything

Profile → Delete Account removes your account and the data stored for it, including anything shared with your partner. Deleting the app from your phone removes the local database along with it. Both are permanent.

Children

Duvo is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the updated date at the top will change. Questions about your data: health.kegel@gmail.com.
